      ## Vector quantisation

      One packed corpus row. Phrase embeddings sit in a high-dimensional
      space. **VQ** (k-means) builds a codebook of `n_vq` **clusters**:
      each cluster is a fine cell of nearby phrases, not a theme. The
      next stage, **04_grain**, reads those centroids and finds
      **lenses** — broader thematic readings of the cells.

      Parameters say `n_vq: 40` (Jess analogue). If the codebook was
      fit under a different knob you will see that cell count here
      (recently 64). Fit VQ overwrites `codebook.parquet`.

      `Q` is the one-hot map phrase → cluster. Grain writes `R`
      (cluster → lens). Phrase-level lens membership is `QR`. Document
      histograms in 05 still count phrases, even when 04 treats each
      cluster as one geometric point.

      ### Projection walk

      The figure is a **Brownian walk of 2-D planes** in the leading-variance
      PCA subspace (not a static PCA scatter, and not the ambient 3072-d
      vectors). Diamonds are codebook **centres** (size ~ √mass). Grey
      ellipses are the **1-SD extent** of member phrases in the current
      plane. **Play** / **Pause** run the walk. When linger is on, the
      step length is `speed / √v` where `v` is variance captured by the
      current plane, so high-variance views linger.
